# Code of conduct

We want project spaces to be useful, respectful, and safe for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be specific and constructive. Discuss code and evidence rather than a person's ability or intent. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use sexualized or degrading language. Respect a contributor's decision to stop a discussion.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and focused on protecting the community.

Report conduct concerns through the maintainer contact method in the repository's GitHub profile. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
